\section{Simple Shear}
Figure~\ref{fig:simple_shear} illustrates {\bf simple shear}, with 
relative motion of planes normal to the vertical axis.
For all configurations (reference and current):
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0em}
\item Horizontal fibers remain horizontal.
\item The length of horizontal fibers remains constant.
\item The vertical distance between the horizontal fibers remains constant.
\item The body's volume is constant. The deformation is isochoric since $\det(\tFbold) = 1$, see 
(\ref{eq:deformation_gradient_iJ}).
\end{itemize}

The relative motion is characterized by the non-dimensional ratio of length $a$ per unit height, 
where $a \in \real \subset [0, \infty)$.
The angle, $\gamma \in \real \subset [0, \pi/2)$, subtended by simple shear is $\gamma = \arctan(a)$.  
In the limit as $a \mapsto \infty$, the shear angle $\gamma \mapsto \pi/2$.
For small values of $a$, the small-angle assumption is valid, with $\tan(\gamma) \approx \gamma \approx a$.

\begin{figure}[htb]
  \begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{fig/simple_shear.pdf}
  \end{center}
  \caption{(Left) {\bf Simple shear} of a unit cube in the reference configuration (gray) and two current configurations (dotted, green and dashed, red) and (right) with the shear angle, $\gamma$, created for all configurations that are parameterized by $a \in [0, 10]$.}
  \label{fig:simple_shear}
\end{figure}

In Fig.~\ref{fig:simple_shear} (left), the horizontal displacement per unit height, $a$, is illustrated by the dotted, green configuration.  The (right) figure also shows the shear angle $\gamma = \arctan(a)$, with the three $(a, \gamma)$ pairs plotted in a color that matches the corresponding deformation in the left figure.

\subsection{Rate-Independent}
The simple shear deformation $\vvarphi$ in rate-independent form is
\be
\vvarphi(\vX) = (X_1 + a X_2) \; \veone + X_2 \; \vetwo + X_3 \; \vethree.
\ee
The deformation gradient $\tFbold$ is
\begin{align}
\tFbold
& = \vesubi \frac{\partial \varphi_i}{\partial X_J} \vEsubJ = \veone \vEone + a \; \veone \vEtwo + \vetwo \vEtwo + \vethree \vEthree
\\
& = \left\{\veone \; \vetwo \; \vethree \right\}
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
1 & $a$ & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right]
\left\{
\begin{tabular}{c}
$\vEone$ \\
$\vEtwo$ \\
$\vEthree$
\end{tabular}
\right\}
\;\;
\Longleftrightarrow
\;\;
\tF_{iJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
1 & $a$ & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:deformation_gradient_iJ}
\end{align}
Note that the volume remains constant for all deformations since $\det(\tFbold) = 1$.  The deformation thus belongs to the group of isochoric motions (see Fig.~\ref{fig:motion_family}).  The right Cauchy-Green strain tensor, $\tCbold = \tFbold \transpose \tFbold$, in simple shear, is
\begin{align}
\tCbold & = \vEone \vEone + a\; (\vEone \vEtwo + \vEtwo \vEone) + (a^2 + 1) \; \vEtwo \vEtwo + \vEthree \vEthree \;\;\Longleftrightarrow
\\
\tC_{IJ} & = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
 1 & $a$ & 0 \\
 $a$ & $a^2+1$ & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:C_IJ_simple_shear}
\end{align}
The right stretch tensor, $\tUbold = \sqrt{\tCbold}$, is
\be
\tU_{IJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
tk0 & tk0 & tk0 \\
tk0 & tk0 & tk0 \\
tk0 & tk0 & tk0
\end{tabular}
\right].
\ee
%
The Green-Lagrange strain tensor, $\tEbold = \half (\tCbold - \vid)$, in simple shear is
\be
\tE_{IJ} = 
\half
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
 0 & $a$ & 0 \\
 $a$ & $a^2$ & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 0
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:E_IJ_simple_shear}
\ee
The left Cauchy-Green strain tensor, $\tbbold = \tFbold \tFbold \transpose$, (and its inverse) in simple shear is
\be
\tb_{ij} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
 $a^2+1$ & $a$ & 0 \\
 $a$ & 1 & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right], 
\;\;\mbox{ and } \;\;
\tb^{-1}_{ji} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
 1 & $-a$ & 0 \\
 $-a$ & $a^2+1$ & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:b_ij_simple_shear}
\ee
The Euler-Almansi strain tensor, $\tebold = \half(\vid - \tbbold^{-1})$, for simple shear is
\be
\te_{ij} = 
\half
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
 0 & $a$ & 0 \\
 $a$ & $-a^2$ & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 0
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:e_ij_simple_shear}
\ee

\subsection{Rate-Dependent}
The simple shear deformation $\vvarphi$ in rate-dependent form is
\be
\vvarphi(\vX, t) = (X_1 + a(t) X_2) \; \veone + X_2 \; \vetwo + X_3 \; \vethree.
\ee
The deformation gradient $\tFbold$ is unchanged from 
(\ref{eq:deformation_gradient_iJ}).  Additionally, the rate and inverse, 
respectively, are found to be
\be
\dot{\tF}_{iJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
0 & $\dot{a}$ & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{tabular}
\right]
\;\;\mbox{ and }\;\;
\tF^{-1}_{Ji} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
1 & $-a$ & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right].
\ee
From these two foregoing quantities, the spatial velocity gradient, 
$\grad \vv = \dot{\tFbold} \tFbold^{-1}$, is
\be
v_{i,j} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
0 & $\dot{a}$ & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{tabular}
\right].
\ee
From this, the rate of deformation tensor, 
$\tdbold = \half[\grad \vv + (\grad \vv)\transpose]$, 
and spin tensor, 
$\twbold = \half[\grad \vv - (\grad \vv)\transpose]$, respectively, are
\be
\td_{ij} = 
\half
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
0 & $\dot{a}$ & 0 \\
$\dot{a}$ & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{tabular}
\right]
\;\;\mbox{ and }\;\;
\tw_{ij} = 
\half
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
0 & $\dot{a}$ & 0 \\
$-\dot{a}$ & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{tabular}
\right].
\ee
The pullback operator, $\pullback{\cdot}$, applied to the rate of deformation
tensor, $\tdbold$, is the time derivative of the Green-Lagrange strain tensor, 
$\dot{\tEbold} = \tFbold\transpose \tdbold \tFbold$.  
This result can be checked by taking time derivatives 
directly of $\tCbold$ and $\tEbold$, respectively found to be
\be
\dot{\tC}_{IJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
0 & $\dot{a}$ & 0 \\
$\dot{a}$ & $2 a \dot{a}$ & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{tabular}
\right]
\;\;\mbox{ and }\;\;
\dot{\tE}_{IJ} = 
\half
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
0 & $\dot{a}$ & 0 \\
$\dot{a}$ & $2 a \dot{a}$ & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{tabular}
\right].
\ee
The rotated rate of deformation tensor, 
$\tDbold = \tRbold\transpose \tdbold \tRbold$, is
\be
{\tD}_{IJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
tk0 & tk0 & tk0 \\
tk0 & tk0 & tk0 \\
tk0 & tk0 & tk0
\end{tabular}
\right],
\ee
and is related to the Green-Lagrange strain rate through 
$\dot{\tEbold} = \tUbold \tDbold \tUbold$ since 
\be
\dot{\tEbold} = \tFbold\transpose \tdbold \tFbold 
= (\tRbold \tUbold)\transpose \;\tdbold\; \tRbold \tUbold 
= \tUbold\transpose \tRbold\transpose \;\tdbold \; \tRbold \tUbold 
= \tUbold\transpose \tDbold \tUbold
= \tUbold \tDbold \tUbold.
\ee
