\section{Simple Shear}
Simple shear is illustrated in Fig.~\ref{fig:simple_shear}, with 
relative motion of planes normal to the $\vEtwo$ axis.
For all configurations (reference and current):
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0em}
\item Horizontal fibers remain horizontal.
\item The length of horizontal fibers remains constant.
\item The vertical distance between the horizontal fibers remains constant.
\item The body's volume is constant. The deformation is isochoric since $\det(\tFbold) = 1$.
\end{itemize}

The relative motion is characterized by the non-dimensional ratio of length $a$ per unit height, 
where $a \in \real \subset [0, \infty)$.
The angle, $\gamma \in \real \subset [0, \pi/2)$, subtended by simple shear is $\gamma = \arctan(a)$.  
In the limit as $a \mapsto \infty$, the angle $\gamma \mapsto \pi/2$.
For small values of $a$, the small small-angle assumption is valid, with $\tan(\gamma) \approx \gamma \approx a$.

\subsection{Rate-Independent}
The simple shear deformation $\vvarphi$ in rate-independent form is
\be
\vvarphi(\vX) = (X_1 + a X_2) \; \veone + X_2 \; \vetwo + X_3 \; \vethree.
\ee
The deformation gradient $\tFbold$ is
\begin{align}
\tFbold
& = \vesubi \frac{\partial \vvarphi_i}{\partial X_J} \vEsubJ = \veone \vEone + a \; \veone \vEtwo + \vetwo \vEtwo + \vethree \vEthree, 
\\
& = \left\{\veone \; \vetwo \; \vethree \right\}
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
1 & $a$ & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right]
\left\{
\begin{tabular}{c}
$\vEone$ \\
$\vEtwo$ \\
$\vEthree$
\end{tabular}
\right\}
\;\;
\Longleftrightarrow
\;\;
\tF_{iJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
1 & $a$ & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:deformation_gradient_iJ}
\end{align}
The right Cauchy-Green strain tensor, $\tCbold = \tFbold \transpose \tFbold$, is
\begin{align}
\tCbold & = \vEone \vEone + a\; (\vEone \vEtwo + \vEtwo \vEone) + (1 + a^2) \; \vEtwo \vEtwo + \vEthree \vEthree, 
\\
& \Longleftrightarrow \;\; \tC_{IJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
 1 & $a$ & 0 \\
 $a$ & $a^2+1$ & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:C_IJ_simple_shear}
\end{align}
The Green-Lagrange strain tensor is simply $\tEbold = \half \tCbold$.

\be
\vvarphi(\vX, t) = (X_1 + a(t) X_2) \; \veone + X_2 \; \vetwo + X_3 \; \vethree.
