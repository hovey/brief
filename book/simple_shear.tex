\section{Simple Shear}
Figure~\ref{fig:simple_shear} illustrates {\bf simple shear}, with 
relative motion of planes normal to the vertical axis.
For all configurations (reference and current):
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0em}
\item Horizontal fibers remain horizontal.
\item The length of horizontal fibers remains constant.
\item The vertical distance between the horizontal fibers remains constant.
\item The body's volume is constant. The deformation is isochoric since $\det(\tFbold) = 1$, see 
(\ref{eq:deformation_gradient_iJ}).
\end{itemize}

The relative motion is characterized by the non-dimensional ratio of length $a$ per unit height, 
where $a \in \real \subset [0, \infty)$.
The angle, $\gamma \in \real \subset [0, \pi/2)$, subtended by simple shear is $\gamma = \arctan(a)$.  
In the limit as $a \mapsto \infty$, the shear angle $\gamma \mapsto \pi/2$.
For small values of $a$, the small-angle assumption is valid, with $\tan(\gamma) \approx \gamma \approx a$.

The (right) figure also shows the shear angle $\gamma = \arctan(a)$, with the three $(a, \gamma)$ pairs plotted in a color that matches the corresponding deformation in the left figure.

\subsection{Rate-Independent}
The simple shear deformation $\vvarphi$ in rate-independent form is
\be
\vvarphi(\vX) = (X_1 + a X_2) \; \veone + X_2 \; \vetwo + X_3 \; \vethree.
\ee
The deformation gradient $\tFbold$ is
\begin{align}
\tFbold
& = \vesubi \frac{\partial \varphi_i}{\partial X_J} \vEsubJ = \veone \vEone + a \; \veone \vEtwo + \vetwo \vEtwo + \vethree \vEthree, 
\\
& = \left\{\veone \; \vetwo \; \vethree \right\}
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
1 & $a$ & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right]
\left\{
\begin{tabular}{c}
$\vEone$ \\
$\vEtwo$ \\
$\vEthree$
\end{tabular}
\right\}
\;\;
\Longleftrightarrow
\;\;
\tF_{iJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
1 & $a$ & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:deformation_gradient_iJ}
\end{align}
Note that the volume remains constant for all deformations since $\det(\tFbold) = 1$.  The deformation thus belongs to the group of isochoric motions (see Fig.~\ref{fig:motion_family}).  The right Cauchy-Green strain tensor, $\tCbold = \tFbold \transpose \tFbold$, is
\begin{align}
\tCbold & = \vEone \vEone + a\; (\vEone \vEtwo + \vEtwo \vEone) + (a^2 + 1) \; \vEtwo \vEtwo + \vEthree \vEthree, 
\\
& \Longleftrightarrow \;\; \tC_{IJ} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
 1 & $a$ & 0 \\
 $a$ & $a^2+1$ & 0 \\
 0 & 0 & 1
\end{tabular}
\right].
\label{eq:C_IJ_simple_shear}
\end{align}
The Green-Lagrange strain tensor is simply $\tEbold = \half \tCbold$.

\be
\vvarphi(\vX, t) = (X_1 + a(t) X_2) \; \veone + X_2 \; \vetwo + X_3 \; \vethree.
