\chapter{Quaternions}

\section{Multiplication}

The multiplication for quaternion bases $i, j, k$ is defined as 
\be
i\;j \defe k, \;\;\; j\;k \defe i, \;\;\;  k\;i \defe j, \;\;\; 
\mbox{and} \;\;\;
 k\;j \defe -i, \;\;\; j\;i \defe -k, \;\;\; i\;k \defe -j.
\ee 

\begin{itemize}
\item The positive combinations of the $i, j, k$ triple can be 
constructed by repeating the $i$ and $j$ at then end of
$i, j, k$ sequence.  Thus, the forward sequence is 
$i, j, k, i, j$.  

\item When two adjacent factors are read
in forward order,\footnote{Likewise, this is the {\em reverse}
alphabetical
order of a repeating triple, $i, j, k, i, j, k, \ldots$} the
result is the next sequential factor, multiplied by $+1$.

\item The negative combinations of the $i, j, k$ triple can be 
constructed by repeating the $j$ and $i$ at then end of
$k, j, i$ sequence.  Thus, the reverse sequence is 
$k, j, i, k, j$.  

\item When two adjacent factors are read
in backward order,\footnote{Essentially, this is alphabetical
order of a repeating triple, $k, j, i, k, j, i, \ldots$} the
result is the next sequential factor, multiplied by $-1$.
\end{itemize}

Organizing the first factor into rows and the second 
factor into columns creates a superposition of a skew matrix
and a negative identity matrix $-\vid$, shown in 
Table~\ref{tab:quat_table}.

\begin{table}[htb]
\label{tab:quat_table}
\caption{Quaternion multiplication table.}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{c||r|r|r|}
    & $i$ & $j$ & $k$ \\ \hline \hline
$i$ & $-1$  & $k$ & $-j$ \\ \hline
$j$ & $-k$ & $-1$ & $i$ \\ \hline
$k$ & $j$ &  $-i$ & $-1$ \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The quaternion matrix $\tQbold$ 
is defined as
\be
\tQbold \defe -\vid - [\vq]_{\times} = 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{rrr}
$-1$ & 0 & 0 \\
0 & $-1$ & 0 \\ 
0 & 0 & $-1$
\end{tabular}
\right]
- 
\left[
\begin{tabular}{rrr}
0 & $-k$ & $j$ \\
$k$ & 0 & $-i$ \\ 
$-j$ & $i$ & 0  
\end{tabular}
\right].
\ee
